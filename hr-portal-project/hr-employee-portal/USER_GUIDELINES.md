# Using the HR Portal

The administrator follows [README.md](README.md) to provision an HR account. Sign in with your email, password and correct role. There are no shared accounts.

HR users can add an employee email and department, view employees, and approve or deny pending leave. After adding an employee, copy the one-time invitation shown in the success message and share it privately with that employee. It is not displayed again in the directory.

New employees choose Sign Up, enter their registered email and invitation code, then provide their first/last name, age (16-120) and password (at least 10 characters). Registration consumes the invitation. Sign in with Employee selected after registration.

Employees can view their own profile, submit a reason and 1-365 whole leave days, and view Pending/Approved/Denied status. Re-enter Leave Status to load the latest HR review decision. HR decisions can be applied once per pending request.

Use Log Out to invalidate your server session. A refresh preserves a valid session; an API restart or eight-hour expiration requires signing in again. If the server cannot be reached, resolve the connection error before retrying an operation.
